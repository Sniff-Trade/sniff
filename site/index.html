What it catches

The tricks that drain wallets.

Printers and freezers

Live mint authority prints more supply. Live freeze authority locks your tokens so you can't sell. Permanent delegates and transfer hooks can take them.

Launch bundles

Wallets that bought in the same block as the creator, and how much of supply they still sit on, waiting for your buy.

The dev's exit

How much the creator bought at launch, what's left in the wallet, and how many other tokens the same wallet has launched before.

Whales

The top 10 holders with the curve and pools taken out, so the number means people, not liquidity.

Copycats

Another token with the same ticker and a market cap many times bigger. The classic fake.

Fake volume

Trading that looks automated rather than human, no trades in 24 hours, thin liquidity after graduation, no socials.

Sniff is a risk check for Solana tokens, built for AI agents and tuned for pump.fun launches. Before an agent buys, it asks Sniff and gets back a score from 0 to 100, a verdict (CLEAN, SUSPECT or ROTTEN) and every reason behind the score.

Why agents need it

Agents are fast and literal. If a token shows momentum, an agent will buy it, even when the freeze authority is live, eleven bundled wallets hold a sixth of supply or the dev already sold. Sniff is the one question an agent should ask before every buy.

What you can use

  • MCP server: one URL for Claude, Cursor and any MCP-capable agent. See MCP server.
  • REST API: GET /v1/check/:mint returns JSON. See REST API.
  • Checker: paste a mint on the home page.

  • Solana RPC: the mint account and its extensions, the pump.fun bonding curve, the 20 largest token accounts and their owners, the launch block's transactions, the dev's and bundled wallets' balances.
  • Jupiter token API: market cap, liquidity, volume, trades, holder count, organic score, the dev's launch history and look-alike tickers.
  • Token metadata file: website and social links set at launch.

Nothing is stored about the tokens or wallets you check. Reports are cached for 60 seconds.

  • The launch block is found by walking back through the curve's history. For tokens with more than 8,000 curve transactions it is skipped, and notes says so.
  • Wallets can be split or funded secretly. Sniff links the dev's wallets through the launch, not through every transfer.
  • Holders come from the 20 largest token accounts, which covers concentration but not the full holder count.
  • A good score is not a promise: price can still fall. Use Sniff to avoid the worst, not to pick winners.

Is a CLEAN token safe?

It has no major red flags in the data Sniff reads. It can still fall in price.

Why did a good project get SUSPECT?

Read the reasons: a dev holding a big bag or a concentrated top 10 is real risk for buyers, even when it's planned. Projects can explain it publicly.

Can a project pay for a better score?

No. The rules are public and the same for every token.

Does Sniff work outside pump.fun?

Yes, for any Solana token: authorities, Token-2022 traps, holders, market and copycats. The launch-block checks are pump.fun only.
